WBRS COS SMS Privacy Policy

WBRS LLC · Effective September 11, 2026

WBRS LLC (WBRS), 265 Brookview Centre Way, Suite 501, Knoxville, TN 37919, sends operational SMS alerts from COS (Chief of Staff) to the franchise owner. This is not marketing.

What we collect

We use the mobile number the owner provided to COS for operational alerts. We also keep delivery records (sent, delivered, failed) so we can see whether a notice went out.

How we use it

We use that number only to send operational alerts: support tickets, ops follow-ups, and related COS notices. We do not sell this data. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS data handling

Messages are sent through Twilio on a registered A2P 10DLC campaign. Recipients can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Opting out stops SMS. It does not stop email or in-app notices.
